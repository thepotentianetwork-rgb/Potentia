A dry run searches your configured cities and stages what it finds. A real run works through the queue: no site, a dead or Facebook-only one, plain http, or one that fails Google's mobile speed test, and they go straight into the list below. It tells you how many it is checking as it starts. The ones with no website are instant; the rest wait on Google's speed test, so a full batch takes several minutes. Everything it finishes is saved even if you close the page.

The list of cities and searches is written once and then left alone, so that runs never lose track of which cities have already been searched. Rebuild it when the city list or the categories have changed — it replaces the whole grid and puts the categories back to their defaults.

Leads found while Google’s speed test was unavailable were never actually speed-tested. Recheck looks at the ones with a real website that nobody has touched yet, keeps the ones that fail, and marks the rest lost so they drop out of your call list.
